Privacy Policy for Dastras (دسترس)
Effective date: October 26, 2026

Dastras (دسترس) is a directory application that helps people find local businesses and services. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the application.
Information we collect
Location selections: You choose your province, city, and district or region to see relevant listings. The application saves these selections on your device. Dastras does not request your device’s precise GPS location.

Information you submit: When you submit a request to add a service or business, we collect the information you enter, which may include your name, phone number, business or service details, selected location, full address, and message.

Technical information: Our infrastructure providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request information, as needed to deliver and secure their services.

You do not need to create an account to browse the directory.
How we use information
We use information to:
- Display businesses and services for your selected location.
- Save your preferences and provide access to downloaded directory data while offline.
- Receive, review, and respond to listing requests.
- Publish approved directory listings.
- Synchronize directory updates and operate the service.
Public directory information
Approved listings are publicly accessible. Information submitted for a listing, including its name, phone number, service details, and address, may become public after administrator approval.

Only submit information you are authorized to share. Do not include sensitive personal information in your request.
Storage and service providers
Dastras uses Google Firebase, including Cloud Firestore, to store and synchronize information. The application also stores directory data and preferences locally on your device.

Pending listing requests may be stored locally so the application can retry sending them when an internet connection is available.

Sharing information
We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared:
- Publicly, when it forms part of an approved directory listing.
- With authorized administrators who review submissions and manage the directory.
- With infrastructure providers needed to operate the service.
- When necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect the service and its users.
Phone calls and external services
If you tap a listing’s phone number, Dastras opens your device’s calling application. You decide whether to place the call. Your mobile provider’s terms and charges may apply.

External websites and services have their own privacy policies.
Data retention
We retain directory listings while they remain relevant to the service. We retain submissions and their review status as needed to process requests, manage listings, and maintain service records.

Locally stored information remains on your device until it is replaced, cleared through your device’s settings, or removed when you uninstall the application. Logging out resets your selected location; it does not delete submitted information from our servers.

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information associated with you. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.
Security
We use safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network connections and access restrictions for administrative functions. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.
Children’s privacy
Dastras is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.
Your choices
You can change your selected location, clear locally stored application data, or uninstall the application.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, contact us at the email below. Include enough information to identify the relevant submission or listing. We may need to verify your connection to that information before acting.
